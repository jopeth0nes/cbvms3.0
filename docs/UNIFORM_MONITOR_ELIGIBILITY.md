Uniform monitoring eligibility
===============================

Uniform monitoring accepts a recognized, non-archived student marked Enrolled
whose registration is verified, even without confirmed academic-term enrollment.
The persistence transaction rechecks this standing before recording a suspected
wrong-uniform violation. Pending registrations, unknown identities and other
standing states do not gain eligibility through this exception.

Reliable face identity, valid face-to-body association, a usable torso crop,
trained model readiness, multi-frame confirmation, stale-frame rejection and
cooldowns still apply. A newly trained model triggers a background readiness retry.

Academic records are preserved. The actual published term is attached when one
exists; otherwise the violation remains unassigned and its appeal is available.
No artificial semester or semester-based strike/suspension is created for a
record without a term. Calendar rules remain separate; live earring checks are retired.
